A little about me

I care about details — the kind most people scroll past without noticing, but feel the absence of when they're missing. My work sits at the edge of design and craft: clean, considered, and never louder than it needs to be.

When I'm not working, I'm usually collecting references, rearranging something that didn't need rearranging, or chasing better light for a photo.
